Kawaii IslandPhase 0A · interactive mockup

01 · Live island, docked as an AppBar

Click the island to expand it, right-click it for the menu, or press Ctrl+Alt+I. It collapses 4 s after the pointer leaves.
The mascot reacts to you. Hover it and its eyes grow. Click it and it squishes and gets annoyed. Click it three times fast and it gets dizzy.
Drag any file from your desktop onto the island. It turns into a box, swallows the file, and adds it to Apps.

02 · States

Compact states put content on the left and status on the right. Each activity has its own color; music takes its color from the album art. Expanded panels are flat on the island background.

05 · Unlocked: drag and snap

Drag the island. If you release it within 24 px of an edge or the center line, it snaps there with a 200 ms ease. A dashed outline shows while it's unlocked.

Unlock to drag

07 · Auto-hide: peek and say hi

When a fullscreen app is detected, the island slides off the top edge in 250 ms and releases the reserved strip. Hover the glowing top edge: the mascot peeks out and says hi 👋. Click it and the island opens. It hides again 800 ms after the pointer leaves.

08 · Mascot sheet

Five original characters, and only one of them is anime. They share the same eyes and mouth, so every reaction works for each one. The extra expressions are wow (hover), annoyed (click) and dizzy (three fast clicks). When you drop a file, the mascot turns into a box.
